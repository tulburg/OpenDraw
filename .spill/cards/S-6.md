## Purpose
Today the canvas only pans, zooms and selects artboards. The user needs to pick any layer on the canvas and move, resize, duplicate and delete it by hand, the way Paper's canvas works, with every gesture undoable.

## Requirements
1. Click selects the topmost layer under the cursor at the current depth: on an empty artboard area, the artboard; otherwise the artboard's direct child. Double-click goes one level deeper; cmd/ctrl + click selects the deepest layer under the cursor directly. Escape selects the parent, then nothing.
2. Shift + click adds or removes a layer from the selection. Dragging on empty canvas draws a marquee that selects the artboards it touches. Cmd/ctrl + A selects all siblings of the current selection.
3. Hovering a layer outlines it; the selection shows its bounds with eight resize handles and a size label (width x height).
4. Dragging a selected artboard or absolutely positioned layer moves it (updates `left`/`top`). Dragging a flex child reorders it among its siblings, with an insertion line where it will drop. Arrow keys nudge positioned layers 1 px, shift + arrow 10 px.
5. Dragging a resize handle sets `width`/`height` in px; shift keeps the aspect ratio. Alt/option resizes from the center.
6. Delete/Backspace deletes the selection; cmd/ctrl + D duplicates it; cmd/ctrl + C and V copy and paste layers (within OpenDraw) into the selected container.
7. Selecting on the canvas and in the layers panel stay in sync; the layers panel expands to and scrolls to the selected layer.
8. Each gesture (a whole drag, a nudge burst, a delete) is one undo step. A gesture in progress never writes to the file until it ends.

## Out of scope
Snapping and smart guides, rotation, drawing new shapes or frames (a later drawing tools spec), grouping, editing in the styles panel (S-7), text editing (S-8).

## Acceptance checks
- Click, double-click, cmd + click and Escape select the expected layer at each depth in a three-level test design.
- Dragging an artboard 100 px right changes its `left` by 100 at any zoom; one undo restores it.
- Dragging the first of three flex children to the end gives the order 2, 3, 1; undo restores 1, 2, 3.
- Resizing with a handle to 300 x 200 writes `300px` and `200px`; shift-resize keeps the ratio within 1 px.
- Delete, duplicate, copy and paste each work and are one undo step.
- Marquee over two of three artboards selects exactly those two; the layers panel shows both selected.
