S-35 makes R and O draw frames with a fill. A frame is a container, so a shape drawn starting on top of another rectangle or circle goes inside it (it is clipped by it and moves with it). In Figma and doop, shapes are leaves: drawing on a rectangle puts the new layer beside it in the same frame or artboard.

A. Shapes are leaves: R and O layers are marked as shapes (a `shape` field on the node, like doop's data-doop-drawing marker), and drawing, pasting or dropping on one puts the layer in the shape's parent. Agents see them as plain divs. Recommended.
B. Keep shapes as frames: anything drawn on them goes inside, as now.
