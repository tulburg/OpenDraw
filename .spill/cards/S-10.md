## Purpose
Resize handles are the only on-canvas controls today. Like Figma, a selected layer should show every handle that applies to it, so the user can rotate, round corners and adjust spacing directly on the canvas. Every handle writes plain CSS, so agents and exported code see the same thing.

## Requirements
1. **Which handles show.** A single selected layer shows the handles that apply to its type, drawn over the selection outline:
   - frames: resize, rotate, corner radius, and padding and gap when it is a flex container;
   - images and SVG layers: resize, rotate, corner radius;
   - text layers: resize and rotate;
   - artboards: resize, corner radius, and padding and gap when flex (no rotation, since artboards are pages, not objects).
   Handles too close together at the current zoom (the layer is under about 24 screen px on a side) are hidden, except resize. Hovering a handle shows the right cursor and a tooltip-style label of the value.
2. **Rotation.** Hovering just outside a corner shows a rotate cursor; dragging rotates the layer around its center. Stored as the CSS `rotate` property (`rotate: 15deg`), which composes with any existing `transform`. Shift snaps to 15 degree steps. A live angle label follows the pointer.
3. **Rotated layers.** The selection outline, all handles and the hover outline follow the rotated shape. Resizing a rotated layer works along its own axes (the opposite edge stays put on screen). Moving and nudging work as for unrotated layers.
4. **Corner radius.** A dot inside each corner; dragging it toward the center sets `border-radius` on all corners (up to half the shorter side). Alt/option + drag sets only that corner (`border-top-left-radius` and so on). A live label shows the radius. Layers with radius show the dots at the radius position.
5. **Padding.** On a flex container, dragging a handle on the inside of each edge sets that side's padding (`padding-top` and so on); alt sets opposite sides together, shift all four. The padding area is shaded while hovering or dragging.
6. **Gap.** On a flex container with two or more children, a handle between children sets `gap` by dragging along the layout direction. The gaps are shaded while hovering or dragging.
7. **Styles panel.** A rotation field (degrees) joins position, with typing, arrow steps and scrubbing as in S-7; corner radius already exists there and stays in sync with the handles.
8. **Agents.** `update_styles` and `write_html` accept `rotate` and `transform: rotate(...)`; `get_computed_styles` and `get_jsx` report what is stored.
9. Each handle drag is one undo step and writes nothing until it ends.

## Out of scope
Rotating several layers around a shared center, flipping, 3D transforms, per-child flex alignment controls, spacing between artboards.

## Acceptance checks
- Selecting a frame, an image, a text layer and an artboard shows exactly the handle sets listed in Req 1.
- Dragging the rotate zone of a 200 x 100 frame 90 degrees writes `rotate: 90deg` (within 1 degree); with shift, an exact multiple of 15. Not available on artboards.
- The outline corners of a 45 degree rotated layer match its rendered corners within 1 px; resizing a 30 degree rotated layer from its east handle keeps its west edge within 1 px on screen.
- Dragging a corner dot 12 px inward writes `border-radius: 12px`; with alt it writes only that corner.
- Dragging the left padding handle of a flex frame 10 px writes `padding-left` 10 px larger; dragging the gap handle 8 px writes `gap` 8 px larger.
- Typing 30 in the rotation field writes `rotate: 30deg`; each handle drag is one undo step, checked at zoom 0.5 and 2.
