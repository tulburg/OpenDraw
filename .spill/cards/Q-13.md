Multiplayer is out (no live cursors, presence or follow), but S-30 lets several accounts edit one file. Doop syncs edits live over a WebSocket; that sync is part of what we would leave out. What should OpenDraw do?

A. Live sync of document changes only (doop's WebSocket document channel), no cursors or presence. Recommended: two editors never overwrite each other, and agents' edits appear live too.
B. Last save wins: each editor loads the file, saves whole, and may overwrite the other's work.
C. One editor at a time: the first to open a file holds an edit lock; others open it read-only until it is released.
