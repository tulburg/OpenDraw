S-32 has ten requirements that touch every part of the editor. Proposed parts, each depending on S-32, which becomes the overview:
1. Positioned layers and shape tools: Req 1 (V H F R O T C), Req 2 (layout model, flex control on and off). Built first; it fixes the flex-by-default problem.
2. Pen and path editing: Req 1 (P), Req 3.
3. Rulers, guides and zoom: Req 4, Req 5.
4. Shortcuts, element menu and search: Req 6, Req 7, Req 8.
5. Themes and artboard version history: Req 9, Req 10.
No questions or decisions are on S-32 yet, so nothing else moves.

A. Split into these five, build part 1 first. Recommended.
B. Keep S-32 whole and build it in order.
