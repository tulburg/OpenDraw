## Purpose
Connect a Slack workspace to an OpenDraw server, copied from doop's Slack integration (server/slack, src/integrations/slack, docs/slack.md). In doop, mentioning @Doop runs the Doop Agent, which we are leaving out, so what a mention does here follows Q-14.

## Requirements
1. Integrations page (server mode) with Slack: install through Slack OAuth v2 with one-time state, show the connected workspace, disconnect. One installer per workspace; another account cannot take it over.
2. The server verifies Slack's request signatures, answers url_verification, and handles app_uninstalled and tokens_revoked by disconnecting.
3. What a mention or `/opendraw <text>` does, and what OpenDraw posts back, follows Q-14.
4. Setup docs adapted from doop's docs/slack.md, with SLACK_CLIENT_ID, SLACK_CLIENT_SECRET and SLACK_SIGNING_SECRET.

## Out of scope
Running a design agent from Slack, Linear, Meta Ads, webhooks.

## Acceptance checks
- With a test Slack app, install from the Integrations page and see the workspace listed; uninstall in Slack disconnects it.
- A request with a bad signature is rejected.
- The behavior chosen in Q-14 works end to end against the test workspace.
