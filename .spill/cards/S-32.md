## Purpose
Bring doop's canvas editor features into OpenDraw (src/components/CanvasDrawing.tsx, PathEditor.tsx, Rulers.tsx, SnapGuides.tsx, ElementMove.tsx, ElementResizeHandles.tsx, FlexLayoutControls.tsx, ElementContextMenu.tsx, CanvasSearchModal.tsx, ZoomControls.tsx, ThemePicker.tsx), and move OpenDraw to doop's layout model: shapes are positioned layers, flex only when the user turns it on.

This is the overview (split by D-13). It owns no requirements; the work is in the five parts.

## How the parts fit
1. Positioned layers and shape tools: built first; the layout model the others draw into.
2. Pen and path editing: paths are positioned layers from part 1.
3. Rulers, guides and zoom: snapping to guides covers moves from part 1.
4. Shortcuts, element menu and search.
5. Themes and artboard version history.

## Out of scope
Prototype mode and interactions, phone and tablet touch handling, presence and follow.

- Every part is done.
