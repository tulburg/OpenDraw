S-20 builds an installable app and DMG. That needs a new dev dependency; nothing in the repo packages Electron today. It is a build-time tool only and does not ship inside the app.

- A. electron-builder: one config in package.json, builds DMG, NSIS and AppImage, widely used (recommended)
- B. Electron Forge: the Electron project's own toolchain, plugin based; more setup to fit our existing Vite and Bun build
- C. @electron/packager plus a separate DMG tool: smallest pieces, but we write the glue ourselves
