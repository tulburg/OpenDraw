## Purpose
The layers panel only lists and selects layers. In Paper it is where you rename, reorder, nest, hide and lock layers, and those edits should not need the canvas or an agent.

## Requirements
1. Double-clicking a layer name renames it in place; Enter commits, Escape cancels, an empty name is refused. One undo step.
2. Dragging layers reorders them among siblings or moves them into another frame or artboard, with a line or a highlighted target showing where they land. Several selected layers move together, keeping their order. Moves that would put a layer inside itself are refused. One undo step.
3. Each row has a hide/show toggle. Hiding sets `display: none` and keeps the layer's previous `display` value so showing restores it exactly; `get_node_info` reports it as not visible. Hidden layers are dimmed in the panel and are not drawn or hit on the canvas.
4. Each row has a lock toggle. A locked layer (and everything inside it) cannot be selected or moved on the canvas, but can still be selected in the panel and edited in the styles panel. Locking is saved with the file and does not change CSS.
5. Frames and artboards collapse and expand; the state is per file. Selecting a layer on the canvas expands its ancestors.
6. Shift+click selects a range of rows; Cmd+click adds or removes one.
7. Right-clicking a row opens a menu: Rename, Duplicate, Delete, Hide/Show, Lock/Unlock, Wrap in frame, Copy as HTML.

## Out of scope
Layer search and filters, layer thumbnails, multi-file drag.

## Acceptance checks
- Renaming a layer by double-click changes its name in the panel and in `get_node_info`; Escape leaves it unchanged; undo restores it.
- Dragging a layer into another frame changes its parent (checked with `get_children`); dragging a frame into its own child does nothing.
- Hiding a `display: flex` frame, then showing it, leaves its style exactly as before; while hidden it is not drawn and `get_node_info` reports it not visible.
- A locked layer cannot be selected by clicking it on the canvas but can be selected in the panel; the lock survives a restart.
- Shift+click selects the rows between two clicks; the context menu's Duplicate and Delete work.
