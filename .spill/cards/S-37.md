## Purpose
Part 3 of S-32 (D-13). Doop's rulers, guides and zoom range (src/components/Rulers.tsx, SnapGuides.tsx, ZoomControls.tsx).

## Requirements
1. Shift+R toggles rulers; dragging out of a ruler adds a guide; guides belong to the page, are saved in the file, undo like any edit, and everything snaps to them within 8 screen pixels.
2. Zoom from 8% to 1600% (doop's range); a pixel grid shows from 300%.
3. The zoom menu adds 50/100/200% presets and the rulers toggle.

## Acceptance checks
- Drag a guide out of the ruler; a dragged frame snaps to it within 8 px; undo removes the guide; it survives reopening the file.
- Zoom stops at 8% and 1600%; the pixel grid appears at 300% and not below.
