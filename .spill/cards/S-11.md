## Purpose
Basic dragging works (S-6), but moving things precisely needs snapping, smart guides and the standard modifiers, and object interactions need broad automated tests so later changes cannot break them unnoticed.

## Requirements
1. Dragging a positioned layer or artboard snaps its edges and center to the edges and centers of its siblings and its parent within 5 screen px, and shows a guide line for each snap. Cmd/ctrl while dragging turns snapping off.
2. Resizing with a handle and drawing with a toolbar tool (S-9) snap the moving edges the same way.
3. While dragging, equal spacing between three or more siblings in a row or column is snapped to and shown with spacing markers.
4. Shift while dragging locks movement to the horizontal or vertical axis, whichever moved more.
5. Alt/option + drag duplicates the selection and moves the copy; the original stays. One undo removes the copy.
6. Dragging several selected layers moves them together, keeping their offsets; snapping uses their combined bounds.
7. Dragging a positioned layer onto another artboard moves it into that artboard, keeping its screen position. Dragging a flex child out of its parent and into another flex container moves it there at the insertion line.
8. A new suite `tests/interactions.test.ts` (run by `bun run test`) covers object interactions end to end at zoom 0.5, 1 and 2: select, move, nudge, resize from each handle, rotate (S-10), reorder, reparent, alt-duplicate, multi-select drag, snapping on and off, and undo/redo of each, checking both the canvas and the saved file.

## Out of scope
Layout grids and rulers, user-placed guides, snapping to other artboards' contents, auto-layout (flex) editing by drag beyond reorder and reparent.

## Acceptance checks
- Dragging a frame to within 3 px of a sibling's left edge lands it exactly aligned and shows a guide; with cmd held it lands where dropped.
- Shift-dragging 100 px right and 20 px down moves only horizontally.
- Alt-dragging a frame leaves the original in place and adds a copy at the drop point; one undo removes the copy.
- Dragging two selected frames 50 px moves both by 50 px and keeps their spacing.
- Dragging a positioned frame from one artboard to another reparents it with no visible jump.
- `tests/interactions.test.ts` passes at all three zoom levels and is part of `bun run test`.
