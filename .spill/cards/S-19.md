## Purpose
Common layout edits are still done by hand or by an agent: wrapping layers in a frame, unwrapping one, aligning and distributing, and changing stacking order. Paper has these as commands with shortcuts, and they map directly onto CSS.

## Requirements
1. Wrap in frame (Cmd+Opt+G) puts the selected sibling layers in a new frame at their position: a flex frame when the parent lays out with flex (the frame takes their place in the flow, with the parent's direction and gap), a positioned frame sized to their bounds when they are absolutely positioned or are artboards' children placed by left/top. The new frame is selected. One undo step.
2. Unwrap (Cmd+Shift+G on a frame) moves its children into its parent at its place and removes it; absolutely positioned children keep their place on the canvas.
3. Align left, center, right, top, middle and bottom work on two or more absolutely positioned layers (or artboards), aligning to their combined bounds; on one layer they align it within its parent. For a flex parent, aligning one child sets `align-self` instead, and the command says so when it cannot apply.
4. Distribute horizontally and vertically spaces three or more positioned layers evenly.
5. Bring forward, send backward, bring to front and send to back (Cmd+], Cmd+[, Cmd+Shift+], Cmd+Shift+[) reorder layers among their siblings.
6. All commands are in an Arrange menu in the menu bar and in the canvas right-click menu, disabled when they do not apply to the selection.

## Out of scope
Tidy up / auto-arrange, alignment to a key object, constraints.

## Acceptance checks
- Wrapping two children of a flex column gives a new frame in their place holding both, and the artboard's screenshot is unchanged except for layer structure.
- Wrapping two absolutely positioned layers gives a frame at their combined bounds, and each keeps its canvas position.
- Unwrapping that frame restores the original structure and positions.
- Aligning three positioned layers left gives them the same `left`; distributing them gives equal gaps.
- Bring to front and send to back change sibling order as `get_children` reports; every command undoes in one step.
