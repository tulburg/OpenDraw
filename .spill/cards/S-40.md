## Purpose
An artboard is the page a design sits on, not a shape on the canvas. Today it takes the same handles and drags as any layer: corner-radius dots, eight resize handles, and a drag on its empty area moves it. Make it behave like a top-level frame in Figma (user, 2 Nov).

## Requirements
1. A selected artboard shows resize handles on its right edge, bottom edge and bottom-right corner only. It has no corner-radius dots and no rotate zones.
2. A drag that starts on an artboard's empty area (or on its background, not on a layer) draws a selection rectangle. Layers inside the artboard that the rectangle touches are selected, as Figma's marquee does inside a frame. Shift adds to the selection.
3. A drag on the artboard's empty area never moves the artboard. Dragging its name label above it moves it, as in Figma; a click on the label selects it.
4. Click on an artboard's empty area selects the artboard (unchanged).
5. Agents keep full control through MCP: update_styles can still set any artboard style.

## Acceptance checks
- Select an artboard: 3 resize handles (e, s, se), 0 radius dots, 0 rotate zones.
- Drag the right handle 100 px: width grows by 100 and left is unchanged; drag the bottom handle: height grows.
- Drag across two of three layers on an artboard's empty area: exactly those two are selected and the artboard has not moved.
- Drag the artboard's name label 100 px right: the artboard moves 100 px; one undo moves it back.
